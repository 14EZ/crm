# Requirements (English summary of the original brief)

## Context
The user is a project manager at a remodeling / restoration construction company in the Inland Empire, California. The company does bathroom and kitchen remodels, painting, asphalt/concrete, electrical and plumbing, and handles insurance claims and city permits.

How a job comes in:
1. A lead comes in by phone, Google or Yelp.
2. A salesperson visits and designs the job with the client.
3. The client gets a price and the contract is signed.

Salespeople work on commission. The PM is salaried and works both in the office and in the field.

Office staff:
- a bookkeeper
- an office person who orders materials and books city inspections
- a receptionist who answers lead calls and does cold calls

Every project has a WhatsApp group with the bosses, the salesperson, the PM, the bookkeeper and the office person.

## The PM's role
The PM is the salespeople's "eyes and hands" in the field and the link between client and contractors:
- talks to clients and keeps them updated
- attends inspections and passes results to contractors and sales
- explains to contractors what needs fixing
- monitors the work and picks up urgent materials
- earns bonuses for Google/Yelp reviews and for recruiting new contractors

## Why an app
WhatsApp alone is messy, and too much has to be remembered or kept in notes. Goals: everything in one place, a consistent process for every job, and not repeating the same mistakes.

Wish list:
- works on iPhone
- add contacts to the phone
- share files from the app
- WhatsApp integration

## Tabs
### Contractors
Editable list with profiles:
- name, phone, license # (if any)
- skills (plumbing, electrical, etc.)
- active and completed jobs
- teammates
- **job limit per team**: crews sometimes take on more than they can handle, and the PM watches this

Contractors who work together can be grouped into teams.

### Projects
Active, completed and **yellow** (preparation stage: waiting on a city permit, an insurance decision or the client) projects. A project has no name; **its address is its name**.

Fields:
- address, client name, client phone, salesperson
- job type, status, contractor(s)
- on/off features: inspections, file uploads, engineering plans

After the job type is chosen, the project should show:
- a typical materials list
- a checklist of things to watch out for
- a completion / handover checklist

Extra: a **tile calculator**. Enter the room and tile dimensions and pieces per box (or area per box), and get the quantity plus 10–15% for cuts.

### Map
A real map showing **active jobs only**. Tap a job to open it. Used to plan the day's route.

Service area (mostly Riverside County): Riverside, Colton, Jurupa Valley, Fontana, San Bernardino, Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, Eastvale, Corona, Norco, Highland, Redlands, Yucaipa, Moreno Valley, Lake Elsinore, Perris, Menifee, Winchester, Murrieta, French Valley, Temecula, Hemet, Beaumont.

### Office
A digital "personal folder" of files, forms and checklists used almost daily:
- completion form (signed by the client to close out a project)
- finance form (when the client financed the project)
- inspection checklists per city
- stage checklists for each job type
- material lists

Anything general that doesn't belong in the other tabs goes here.
